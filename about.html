Real footage. Real people. Real impact.

I believe the most powerful videos aren't the most polished ones. Raw, authentic footage that shows the real people behind a brand is what actually connects with an audience.

I've been editing for 3 years now. I started out doing event coverage and corporate campaigns, which meant being part of the full production process, not just sitting behind a screen. I also have a background in basic to intermediate graphic design, so I always think about the full visual picture.

I'm based in the Philippines and I work with Australian brands to help them tell stories that feel genuine, through reels, brand content, and event coverage.

Outside of work, I run. It helps me clear my head and keeps the creative ideas coming. I'm also a huge music fan, though strangely enough I can't have it playing while I edit. The video's audio needs my full focus.
