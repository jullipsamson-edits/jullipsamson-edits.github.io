Hey, I'm Julli 👋

I'm a Creative Video Content Specialist based in the Philippines, working with Australian brands to help them tell stories that actually feel real.

I've been editing for 3 years — starting out in event coverage and corporate campaigns, which meant being involved in the full production process, not just the edit. I also bring a background in basic to intermediate graphic design, so I think about the full visual picture.

I believe raw, authentic footage is often the most powerful. Amateur shots with heart will always connect with an audience more than something overly polished. The people behind a brand are its biggest asset — my job is to show that.

When I'm not working, I'm running. It clears my head and keeps the ideas flowing. I'm also a big music lover — though funnily enough, I can't listen to it while I edit. The video's audio gets my full attention.
