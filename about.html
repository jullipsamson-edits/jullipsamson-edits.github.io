Real footage. Real people. Real impact.

I believe the most powerful videos aren't the most polished ones. Raw, authentic footage — the kind that shows the real people behind a brand — is what actually connects with an audience.

I've been editing for 3 years, starting out in event coverage and corporate campaigns. That meant being involved in the full production process, not just the edit. I also bring a background in basic to intermediate graphic design, so I think about the full visual picture.

Based in the Philippines, I work with Australian brands to help them tell stories that feel real — through reels, brand content, and event coverage.

When I'm not editing, I'm running. It clears my head and keeps the ideas flowing. I'm also a big music lover — though funnily enough, I can't listen to it while I edit. The video's audio gets my full attention.
